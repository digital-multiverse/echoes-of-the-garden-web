The Bestiary

28 species across four ecosystems: Roots and Ash at Early Access launch, Garden and Cave in its first two updates. Each one lives once.

Roots of the World

  • Earth Caterpillar Slow until it isn't.
  • Elder Centipede It navigates by sensing the decay of other creatures.
  • Moss Lizard The first to arrive.
  • Peat Slug Slower than the moss it lives on.
  • Root Beetle It spends most of its life underground, following root paths that have existed longer than the biome.
  • Root Mycelium It doesn't move.
  • Stone Tortoise No one has ever seen it move at speed.

Tide of Ash

  • Ash Drake Old for this biome — unheard of, really.
  • Ash Salamander Born from ember, it rarely lasts a season.
  • Ash Scorpion Most ash creatures race through their lifecycle.
  • Char Mole The only ash creature that goes deeper instead of faster.
  • Ember Falcon It never lands.
  • Flame Wasp Individually, inconsequential.
  • Smoke Serpent Hard to see against the ashen ground.

Suspended Garden — first Early Access update

  • Crystal Dragonfly Transparent from certain angles.
  • Ethereal Butterfly Its wings shouldn't keep it aloft.
  • Mist Serpent It prefers the space between stages — the moment after one thing ends and before the next begins.
  • Moon Moth It only fully feeds at the slowest part of the night.
  • Silk Spider It builds continuously, even when there is nowhere left to build.
  • Suspended Hummingbird It moves constantly, but at high enough frequency that the effect is stillness.
  • Veil Jellyfish It drifts rather than swims.

Luminous Cave — second Early Access update

  • Abyss Polyp Anchored to the deepest point of the cave floor.
  • Abyss Serpent It comes from somewhere below the known cave.
  • Cave Scorpion It doesn't move unless it has to.
  • Crystal Crab Its shell has grown around the quartz deposits it rests against.
  • Light Fish It generates its own light, which it uses to navigate tunnels that have no other source.
  • Phosphor Eel Leaves a trail of light as it moves.
  • Quartz Snail Each generation is slightly larger than the last.

Echoes of the Garden is a free-to-play idle game about creatures that die for good.
