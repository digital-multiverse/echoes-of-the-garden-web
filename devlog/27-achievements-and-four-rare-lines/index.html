27 achievements and four rare lines

30 October 2026 · Build v0.10.0

This build gives the moment of death more weight.

The Death Ceremony now shows each species' own death text. Four rare lines can also appear there, each one at most once per save. I won't list them.

Read what the Ash Drake leaves behind.

Also in v0.10.0

  • 27 achievements, kept in the game for now and ready for Steam. Three of them count time spent with the garden in focus.
  • All creature lore, recipes and fragments are now in Spanish and Italian too.
  • The grid centres itself in any window size.
  • Elders look old. Their sprites fade and their names change colour by stage.
  • Messages queue. A single death could unlock several things at once, and only the last message survived on screen.

I also fixed a quiet bug: a creature's age ignored the time you were away, so the Book of Echoes reported ages that were far too young.
