Dev log

A garden that remembers

29 October 2026 · Build v0.9.0

If death is the point of the game, the dead should stay on the record. Three builds went into that idea.

  • The Book of Echoes keeps every creature that lived: its name, its generation, how long it lasted and what you chose for it.
  • Lifetime statistics count everything the garden has been through.
  • Eight lore fragments unlock as you play and start to explain what this place is. The intro hints at it too, and I'm keeping the rest for players to find.

The rest of these builds

New players now learn that death is the goal from a short guided first life. Settings gained separate volumes for master, ambient and effects. The interface is available in English, Spanish and Italian. Builds go to itch.io for Web, Linux and Windows.

The art pass drew all 14 species of the two later biomes across their three life stages. While capturing real frames for it I found that the roots, smoke and veils of the backdrops had been invisible for months, drawn underneath the background they were meant to decorate. A contact sheet of the sprites had never shown it. I check art in the running game now.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
