15 November 2026, v0.19.0

A small build with no changes to how the game plays.

A start of its own

The game used to open on the engine's logo. Now it opens on its own: the sprout and the title, on the garden's dark background. The browser version shows the same image while it loads.

Also in v0.19.0

  • Settings has a Licenses page. It lists the engine and the fonts the game is built with, and the licenses they come under.
  • The Windows and Linux builds use the garden's sprout as their icon.
  • The Spanish and Italian achievement names were revised.

If you are testing v0.18.0, nothing changes: the questions are the same.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
