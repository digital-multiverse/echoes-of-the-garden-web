Creatures that die for good

5 July 2026 · Build v0.3.0

I started Echoes of the Garden on 4 June 2026 with one rule: creatures die for good. A month later, that rule is playable.

Each creature goes through seed, hatchling, adult and elder. Then it dies and you choose. Rebirth leaves an Inherited Seed that carries its line forward. Preservation keeps it as a Relic. You get one or the other.

What v0.3.0 can do

  • Two biomes are playable: Roots of the World and Tide of Ash.
  • Each biome has 12 slots in a 4 by 3 grid, and you move creatures by dragging them.
  • Neighbours matter. An affinity wheel decides which creatures work well side by side.
  • The time of day a creature is born sets its affinity.

All 28 species exist as data, though only the first two biomes are in the game for now. The sprites are placeholder pixel art.

The next job is the economy: what creatures produce, what it buys, and how long a collection should take.
