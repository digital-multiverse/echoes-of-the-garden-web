Dev log

One entry per milestone.

  • 14 November 2026, v0.18.0

    What a relic keeps

    A small build: the Sell window says what stays when a relic goes, a newly discovered species shows itself at the edge of the garden, and the clash glow is ember instead of red.

  • 14 November 2026, v0.17.0

    A shorter first life

    Your first creature now lives about ten minutes instead of twenty, and every species in the first two biomes has its nature.

  • 13 November 2026, v0.16.0

    Signs before names

    Species you have not met yet now leave a sign at the Seed Altar, and the Spanish and Italian text had another pass.

  • 13 November 2026, v0.15.0

    Someone to show you around

    Two testers said the first minutes left them with nothing to do and no idea what the game was about. v0.15.0 adds a short guide for the first session.

  • 13 November 2026, v0.14.0

    Every species has a nature

    Species now reveal their nature at their first death, one of them saves everything for the end, and the garden's surplus finally has somewhere to go.

  • 8 November 2026, v0.13.0

    What the first stranger taught me

    The first external tester thought the garden had stopped working. It hadn't, but nothing on screen said so. What changed in v0.13.0.

  • 5 November 2026, v0.12.1

    Ready for strangers

    A design review found eleven quiet minutes in the first session. What changed before the first external playtest.

  • 30 October 2026, v0.10.0

    27 achievements and four rare last words

    Achievements, words at each death, all the lore in three languages, and a bug that made creatures younger than they were.

  • 29 October 2026, v0.9.0

    A garden that remembers

    The Book of Echoes records every creature that lived, the lore arrives, and the game speaks Spanish and Italian.

  • 28 October 2026, v0.6.0

    The bug that paid 60 times too much

    A balance simulator caught a 60x production bug. The economy phase is closed, and the Steam Market moves after Early Access.

  • 5 July 2026, v0.3.0

    Creatures that die for good

    The first playable loop: a creature is born, grows old and dies, and you choose what stays of it.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
