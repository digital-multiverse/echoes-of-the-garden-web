Ready for strangers

5 November 2026 · Build v0.12.1

Before anyone new plays the game, I reviewed v0.10.0 on a fresh save in real time. The first creature, which lives fast on purpose, reached adulthood at 3:03, became an elder at 14:15 and died at 19:51. Between minutes 3 and 14 the player had nothing to do except watch, and nothing said the garden keeps living while the game is closed. That promise is the whole contract of an idle game.

What changed in v0.11 and v0.12

  • First session. The game now states that promise when the first creature grows up, and tells you when you can afford a second seed.
  • What's next. A line under your resources names the next event, such as a creature's next stage or the seed you can now buy.
  • Coming back. The return summary is centred and uses whole numbers, the return bonus shows a countdown, and creatures that grew while you were away carry a small green sprout.
  • Reading the garden. Hover a creature or a buried relic to see its stage, its time left and its affinity.
  • Less noise. A first death used to fire six messages. Achievements, fragments and recipes now share one line.
  • Sound. A low bell plays when you return to deaths waiting for you, and a soft shimmer when a new species stirs.
  • Accessibility. There is a reduced motion option and three text sizes. Stage names carry a glyph as well as a colour, and clashing neighbours show a spiked glow, so colour is no longer the only cue.

This site also moved to echoesofthegarden.com, and the in-game bug reports now arrive through it.

Next comes the external playtest: new players, no guidance, and notes on where they get lost or bored.
