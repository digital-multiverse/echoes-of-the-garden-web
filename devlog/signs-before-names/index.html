Signs before names

13 November 2026, v0.16.0

Until now a species you had not met yet was three question marks at the Seed Altar. Nothing told you it was there, or gave you a reason to wait for it.

Each of those rows now carries one line: a sign that something lives nearby, without its name. In the Roots of the World, one of them reads: "One of the boulders is not where it was last season." There is one for each of the 28 species, in English, Spanish and Italian.

Also in v0.16.0

  • The Spanish and Italian text added in the last three builds had a full review: the natures, Attunement, the first-steps guide and the new lore fragments.

Next I wait for the testers. What they say about the first minutes decides whether the game's pace changes.
