Dev log

Someone to show you around

13 November 2026 · Build v0.15.0

The second tester put it plainly: in the first minutes there was nothing to do, the next thing took too long to unlock, and a little guidance would have helped them understand what the game was about. The first tester had hit a similar wall a week earlier.

I timed the first session against the game's own numbers. The first seed becomes affordable at around two and a half minutes. After the third purchase, at about eleven minutes, nothing points you anywhere until your first creature dies, close to the twenty-minute mark.

First steps

A small card in the corner now walks you through the first session, one step at a time. Each step names something you can do right now and says in one line why it matters:

  • Name your first creature.
  • Open the Codex.
  • Plant a second seed at the Seed Altar.
  • Look at the Affinity Wheel.
  • Plant a species you have not grown yet.
  • Stay for the end of your first creature.

The card closes with the first fate, and you can dismiss it at any time. Players who have already been through a first death never see it.

Also in v0.15.0

  • Four new lore fragments, earned through Attunement. I won't quote them.
  • Reloading a save no longer announces fragments and achievements you already had.

The guide does not change the game's pace. If the first minutes still feel slow with it, the next step is the pace itself.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
