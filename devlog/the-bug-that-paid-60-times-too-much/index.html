The bug that paid 60 times too much

28 October 2026 · Build v0.6.0

Before building any crafting screen I wrote a headless simulator that plays the economy for weeks of game time. It found a critical bug: production rates were designed per minute and the code paid them per second. Everything earned 60 times too much, and every resource sat at its cap.

With that fixed, a simulated player checking in twice a day owns every Roots and Ash species around day 4.4, and reaches the fifth crafting tier in Tide of Ash around day 24.

What this phase added

  • Encounters and the Seed Altar. New species reveal themselves as you play, and you buy their seeds at the Altar.
  • The Echo Arc. Five crafting tiers, from Vestigio to Eco, each with a risk of destroying what you put in.
  • Mutations. They now change how fast a creature produces and how long it lives, and they tint its sprite.
  • Offline progress. A creature that lived and died while you were away used to come back with nothing to its name. Its output now counts.

A change of plan

Early Access will launch without the real Steam Market, which becomes the first major update after launch. Until then, selling a relic pays out inside the game. The Suspended Garden and the Luminous Cave arrive as the first two Early Access content updates.

Echoes of the Garden is a free-to-play idle game about creatures that die for good.
