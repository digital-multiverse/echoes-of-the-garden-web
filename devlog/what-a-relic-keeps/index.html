What a relic keeps

14 November 2026, v0.18.0

A small build, mostly polish.

Selling a relic

The Sell window now says what a sale takes and what it leaves. The relic leaves your garden. Its name and the life it lived stay in the Book of Echoes.

Also in v0.18.0

  • When you discover a species, it appears for a moment at the edge of the garden, then fades.
  • Creatures that clash with a neighbour now glow ember instead of red. The red looked like an error.
  • Panels that open at the side are taller and now reach down to the bottom bar.

The testers still decide what comes next.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
