Dev log

What the first stranger taught me

8 November 2026, v0.13.0

The first external tester played the web build with no guidance. The first creature lived and died as planned. Then the garden went quiet: the other creatures only gave resources every five minutes, so for a while nothing seemed to happen, and the tester decided production was broken.

The numbers were right. The game just never showed them. Two more things came out of the same session: messages faded before anyone read them, and nothing said whether the browser version keeps your progress. It does.

What changed in v0.13.0

  • A gift every minute. Creatures now give every minute instead of every five, at the same rate per hour. The garden earns exactly what it did before, and you can see it working.
  • The pace, explained. After the first creature's fate, a short note says the others grow over hours and keep giving while the game is closed.
  • Next gift. Hover a creature to see when its next gift arrives and how much it brings.
  • Messages you notice. Messages appear as cards with a soft sound. The important ones stay until you click them.
  • Unread mail. The Mail button counts the messages you haven't opened yet.
  • Saving on the web. The browser version says once that your progress is saved in that browser.

The web build also lost a few symbols that its font could not draw.

One tester is not enough to tune the game's pace, so the next step is more players.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
