# Design intent — Echoes of the Garden website

Read by the `design` and `design-review` skills (`~/.claude/skills/`). Migrated from the
old repo-local `design-review` skill on 2026-11-08.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What it is
The website of Echoes of the Garden (echoesofthegarden.com), a free idle creature collector
where every creature lives, ages and dies for good, leaving a relic. Not the game itself.
A first-time visitor should get, from the first screen: what the game is, what you do in
it, that it's free and playable now in the browser, and that Steam Early Access is coming.

## Audience
Players who arrive from Google (creature and dev log pages are the most indexed: 28
creatures × 3 languages + dev log entries), from itch.io and from word of mouth. Mobile
and desktop both matter. Languages: English first, then Spanish and Italian.

## Primary jobs and CTAs

| CTA | Target | Must appear on |
|---|---|---|
| Play free in browser | https://echoes-of-the-garden.itch.io/play | Home, every creature page, every dev log entry, `/bestiary/`, 404 |
| Get notified / Subscribe | `/#newsletter` (form → `/api/subscribe` in `src/worker.js`) | Home, every creature page, every dev log entry |
| Steam (wishlist) | Not live yet: shown as plain text "Steam (soon)", never a dead link | Footer; becomes a CTA when the Steam page exists (BACKLOG W3) |

## Promises: what we can say
- Free to play, playable now in the browser on itch.io (live).
- 28 creatures in 4 ecosystems; permanent death; relics that carry the creature's name and life (game README).
- No pay-to-win and no real-money shop: every item comes from play (game README).
- Steam Early Access target: Q2 2027 (game ROADMAP, ADR-012).
- Placeholder pixel art in a real game, no mock-ups (stated on the site).

## What we must not say
- **Any trading or Steam Community Market as available today.** Early Access ships without
  the Market; it comes with the first major update after EA (game `docs/adr/012-*`).
  "Market value", "tradeable item" in present tense, or a "Trading" nav entry are findings.
- **"Nothing is sold for real money"** without qualification: Market sales are for Steam
  wallet funds, which is real money. Say "no real-money shop" instead.
- **The Gardener** is not named on the site; the frame is revealed in the game through play
  (game `docs/adr/013-*`).
- "No cookies": Cloudflare JS Detections sets `cf_clearance` (decided 2026-11 to keep it;
  privacy page names it).
- Release dates more precise than the game ROADMAP supports, or a date the ROADMAP has moved.

## Brand
- Voice: quiet, elegiac, plain. Sounds like a gardener's notebook; never sounds like a
  gacha ad ("Collect them all!", "Epic loot!").
- Type: Cinzel Decorative (display), Crimson Pro (body), JetBrains Mono (small data),
  self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` (latin subset, OFL). Every page loads `/assets/fonts/fonts.css` with the same
  two preloads; no Google Fonts request.
- Colour tokens: `:root` in `index.html` (~line 78); dev log, creature pages and privacy
  share them through `scripts/site-chrome.mjs`.
- Imagery: real in-engine captures only (source: `marketing/steam` in the game repo); the
  hero loop is `garden_loop.mp4` + poster. Never stock art, never AI-generated "concept art".
- Emoji in CTAs (🌱) are part of the voice, used sparingly.

## Languages
`/` (en), `/es/`, `/it/`, generated by `scripts/build-i18n.mjs` from the English pages.
Switcher "EN · ES · IT" in the nav and in the mobile menu (`NAVLANG` block). Species names
stay in English on purpose, as in the game (`KEEP` list in `build-i18n.mjs`). Translations
are drafts until a native speaker checks them (BACKLOG W4). The welcome email is English only
(BACKLOG).

## Links and surfaces
- Canonical: https://echoesofthegarden.com/. `www.` and `echoesofthegarden.es` must 301 to it.
  The old Netlify site (`echoes-of-the-garden.netlify.app`) was switched off on 2026-11-10: its
  links return 404 and no longer redirect.
- itch.io page should link back to the site (manual action in BACKLOG).
- Legal: `/privacy/` holds the LSSI-CE owner data; footer "Legal notice" → `/privacy/#who-we-are`.

## Stack and where to fix things
Static HTML/CSS/JS on Cloudflare Workers static assets; Workers Builds deploys every push
to `main`. Headers/CSP in `_headers`. Fix findings in the sources, not in generated HTML:
- Home: `index.html` (hero, nav, sections, newsletter, footer, JS observers at the end).
- Creature pages and `/bestiary/`: `scripts/build-bestiary.mjs` (needs the game checkout).
- Dev log: `scripts/build-devlog.mjs` + `scripts/devlog-entries.mjs`.
- Shared header/CTA block: `scripts/site-chrome.mjs`. Translations: `scripts/build-i18n.mjs`.
- API (subscribe, unsubscribe, report): `src/worker.js`.
Cloudflare settings go through the `infra-servidores` skill.
