How to play

Chapter 1 of 5

A life, from seed to elder

Tend what grows. Let it end. Then choose what remains.

You start in the Roots of the World with one creature already planted. It is your First Echo, and it lives a whole life in minutes so that you see one from start to end on your first visit.

Four stages

Every creature goes from Seed to Hatchling, then Adult, then Elder, and then it dies. It can't be stopped or undone. While it lives it gives its garden a little of that garden's resource each minute: Root Sap in the Roots of the World, Living Ash in the Tide of Ash.

The others keep the garden's own pace: they grow over hours, not minutes. Slow species live longer and give steadily; the creatures of the Tide of Ash burn through their lives quickly.

Natures

Every species has a nature. You learn it the first time one of them dies, and from then on the Codex shows it in that species' entry.

Most species are Steady: they give at the same pace from the day they hatch to the day they die. A Bloomer gives little while it lives and pays it all back when it dies, at the moment you choose its fate. More natures will follow.

Planting

Each garden has twelve spaces in a grid of four by three. You plant seeds bought at the Seed Altar or inherited from a creature that died. Where you put them matters, and the chapter on affinities explains why.

While you are away

The garden keeps living while you are away. When you come back, what it gathered is waiting, the creatures have grown, and one may have died. A creature that dies while you are gone does not disappear: it waits for you to choose what it leaves behind.

In the browser version your garden is saved in the browser itself. Come back in the same one, not in a private window.
