How to play

Chapter 2 of 5

When a creature dies

This is what the garden is for. Nothing that ends is wasted.

When a creature reaches the end of its life the garden falls silent. You see how long it lived and what it gave, and then you choose what remains of it. Your first death also opens the second ecosystem, the Tide of Ash.

Rebirth

Its echo takes seed. You get an Inherited Seed that waits in your Inventory; plant it and the line goes on, one generation further. A rebirth can add a mutation, and the line carries every mutation it has gathered. Mutations change a little how fast a creature grows or how much it gives.

Preservation

Its form endures and its line ends. You get a Relic. One relic per garden is active at a time and strengthens the whole garden; the rarer the relic, the more it helps. Long lines with many mutations are more likely to leave rare relics.

A preserved relic stays buried in the space where its creature lived, so that space stays taken. You can also sell a relic for its garden's resource.

What every death leaves

Whichever fate you choose, every death also leaves a Vestigio, the first material of the Echo Arc. And every life, reborn or preserved, is written in the Book of Echoes, with its name, its generation and how it ended.
