How to play

The Echo Arc

Refine loss into the Echo Arc, from Vestigio to Origen.

Every death leaves a Vestigio of its garden. The Echo Arc is where you turn those remains into something more.

The tiers

Several Vestigios combine into one Rastro, several of those into an Impronta, and so on up the arc. There are ten tiers in all:

  1. Vestigio
  2. Rastro
  3. Impronta
  4. Murmullo
  5. Eco
  6. Resonancia
  7. Pulso
  8. Aliento
  9. Voz
  10. Origen

The first five are in the game now. The other five arrive in later updates.

Refining

From Murmullo on, each attempt also needs a refined material. Refining turns a garden's resource into it: Root Sap into Ancient Root Echo, Living Ash into Ash Ember.

The risk

The first three tiers always work. From Murmullo on, an attempt can fail and destroy what you put in, and the higher you go the more likely that is. Reaching Eco, the fifth tier, is the milestone the game is named after.

Attunement

A garden can only hold so much of its resource, and late in the game most of what it makes would go to waste. For that, the Echo Arc gives the Roots of the World and the Tide of Ash an Attunement each.

Offer pours all the resource you hold into that garden's Attunement. What you offer carries over from one rank to the next, and each rank leaves a Rastro.
