How to play

Five short chapters, in the order the game shows them to you.

  1. A life, from seed to elder

    Planting, the four stages of a life, and what the garden does while you are away.

  2. When a creature dies

    The death ceremony, the choice between Rebirth and Preservation, and what every death leaves.

  3. Where to plant

    The six affinities, how neighbours help or hinder each other, and why the hour of birth matters.

  4. New species

    How a species you have not met shows itself, and when the Seed Altar will sell you its seed.

  5. The Echo Arc

    Refining, the ten tiers from Vestigio to Origen, and the risk of losing what you put in.

Echoes of the Garden is a free idle game about creatures that live, age and die for good.
