Where to plant

Neighbours on the wheel resonate. Opposites dissonate.

Every creature carries one resonance. There are six, in a ring: Arraigo, Ascua, Fulgor, Brisa, Escarcha and Umbra, and after Umbra the ring closes on Arraigo again.

Neighbours

Only the spaces touching a creature count: above, below, left and right. Two creatures whose resonances sit next to each other on the ring both produce more. Two whose resonances are opposite both produce less. Anything further apart stays quiet.

Opposites
Arraigo and Brisa
Ascua and Escarcha
Fulgor and Umbra

The garden shows you the result: a gold glow on a creature in synergy with its neighbours, a red spiked glow on one clashing with a neighbour. The Affinity Wheel, in the game's Settings, draws the ring.

The hour of birth

A species does not always have the same resonance. It depends on the part of the day it was born in: night, morning, midday or evening. It is set at birth and never changes. So the moment a creature is born also decides where it fits best.
