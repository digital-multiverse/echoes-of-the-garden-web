How to play

Chapter 4 of 5

New species

Trade what the garden gave you for new life.

Nothing new is for sale at the start. The garden shows you a creature before it will trade you its seed.

Something stirs

As a garden earns its resource, a species you have not met yet shows itself, with a message such as “Something stirs in the Roots of the World”. Its entry opens with its name, what kind of life it lives and a few lines about it. There is no chance involved: keep the garden producing and every species of it appears in turn.

The Seed Altar

Once a species has shown itself, the Seed Altar sells its seed for that garden's resource. Each new species costs more than the one before, so the later ones take a while. Species you have not met yet appear there veiled, and cannot be bought.

Seeds from the altar start a new line at the first generation. To keep a line going, choose Rebirth when the creature dies.

Forms no seed sells

Two more forms exist that the altar never offers. Their recipes are learned by playing, from the fates you choose, and the Codex keeps them once learned.

The Codex

Every species you meet goes into the Codex. As you play, the garden also sets aside fragments of its own story there.
