Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This site has no advertising, no analytics and no cookies. We only handle personal data in the cases below.

Who we are

Echoes of the Garden is a game by Digital Multiverse. The owner of this site and the data controller is José Javier Pardines García, NIF 53243163A, 33006 Oviedo (Asturias, Spain), info@digital-multiverse.com.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address to send you news about the game, the Early Access launch and the Founder's Seed. We rely on your consent, and we keep your address until you unsubscribe.

To unsubscribe, write to hello@echoesofthegarden.com. We remove you by hand, so it can take a few days.

Bug reports from the game

If you use the report option in the game, we receive your message, a copy of your save file, the build and your platform. We use them only to fix the problem, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Technical data

Your IP address is used to serve the site and, for up to ten minutes, to limit abuse of the forms. We do not store it.

Who else handles this data

We use Cloudflare to host the site and Resend to store subscribers and send email. Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. These providers may process data outside the European Union. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to hello@echoesofthegarden.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority, which in Spain is the AEPD.
